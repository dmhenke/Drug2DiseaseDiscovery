How far would each compound move an SLE cell population toward healthy? 140 compounds measured in OP3 · commit d805845 · All reports

Match to healthy is 1 − ‖disease + drug‖ / ‖disease‖ across the population's SLE signature genes. 1 means the signature is fully closed, 0 means no change, and below 0 means further from healthy.

This is a calculation, not an observation. No lupus cell here was treated. Drug effects were measured in cultured healthy PBMCs and added to the disease signature, which assumes the two effects add. OP3's coarse cell types stand in for finer populations: both monocyte groups use Myeloid. Use it to choose compounds to test, not as evidence that a drug works in lupus.

Read match alongside specific reversal. Most compounds shift these genes the same way, for a reason not yet known, so a good match can be unremarkable. A strong drug can also score badly by overshooting: the sum pushes genes past healthy, which may not happen in a real cell.

Reading the columns

  • Match to healthy has a 95 % interval from resampling signature genes. It reflects which genes are in the signature, not donor or experimental variation.
  • Specific reversal runs from −1 to 1. It is the alignment with the opposite of the disease signature after removing the median compound's effect, with the compound's rank beside it. It ignores effect size. It was added after seeing the first results, so it is exploratory.
  • Size vs disease is the drug's effect on the signature genes divided by the signature's own size. Above 1, the drug moves these genes more than the disease does and match to healthy starts to penalise it.
  • Off-target is the root-mean-square log2 change on genes outside the signature. A high value next to a good match can mean a broadly disruptive or toxic compound rather than a specific one.
  • Lupus treatment status comes from guidelines, the US label, PubMed and ChEMBL, checked 2026-11-05. “Current” includes off-label use and, where the note says so, evidence for the drug class or for one manifestation only. “Trials only” is not an established treatment. No marker means none was found in this check, not that the drug was never used. Open a row for the basis and source.
  • Evidence is “measured” for every row: OP3 profiled the compound. Structure-based predictions are not shown, because the Stage P model failed its test.
Diagnostic tool, not a result for publication and not treatment guidance. Drug data: OP3, Szalata et al., NeurIPS 2024 Datasets and Benchmarks (GSE279945), CC BY 4.0. Disease data: Perez 2022 cohort 4, 20 vs 20 donors.
